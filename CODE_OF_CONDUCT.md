# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participants should communicate constructively, assume good intent, respect differing viewpoints, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, threats, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's configured security or maintainer contact. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
